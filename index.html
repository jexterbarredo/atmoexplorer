Thermosphere

Altitude: 86 to 372 miles

The edge of space. Exposed to intense solar radiation, temperatures here can soar from 930°F to 3600°F. The ISS orbits in this layer.

☄️

Mesosphere

Altitude: 32 to 85 miles

The deep freeze. This is the coldest atmospheric layer, burning up millions of meteors every day before they reach the surface.

✈️

Stratosphere

Altitude: 11 to 31 miles

The protective shield. Houses the vital Ozone Layer which absorbs harmful UV radiation, blocking up to 99% before it reaches Earth.

☁️

Troposphere

Altitude: 0 to 10 miles

The surface layer. Holds 75% of the atmosphere's mass and is home to all weather — storms, clouds, and the air we breathe every day.

💡 Lesson Wrap-Up

Generalization

Let's put it all together! Tap each question to reveal the answer.

1
What are the 5 layers of the atmosphere?
☁️ 1. Troposphere
✈️ 2. Stratosphere
☄️ 3. Mesosphere
🌌 4. Thermosphere
🛸 5. Exosphere
2
What is the importance of the atmosphere for us human beings?
Possible Answer:

The atmosphere is important for life here on Earth because it provides carbon dioxide for plants and oxygen for animals and us, humans.

It also shields us from harmful UV radiation, keeps our planet at a livable temperature, and protects us from space debris like meteors.

3
What happens if too much pollution builds up in the atmosphere?
Possible Answer:

It can cause health problems, like asthma and other respiratory illnesses for people who breathe the polluted air.

Too much pollution can also damage the ozone layer, allowing more harmful UV rays to reach Earth's surface, and contributes to climate change.
